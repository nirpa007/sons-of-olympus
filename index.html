COMBAT BOOK

  • RHYTHM COMBOSPress HIT again just as the swing ends - not as fast as you can. In rhythm = PERFECT TIMING (+20% damage). Smashing the button = TOO FAST, the combo breaks and you stumble.
  • HIT, HIT, HIT, HITYour style's light combo (the Assassin has 5 hits, the Berserker 3 huge ones).
  • HIT then HEAVYLAUNCHER: throws the monster into the air and you jump after it. Then HIT, HIT, HIT in the air = AIR COMBO, HEAVY = smash down.
  • HIT, HIT then HEAVYYour style's special move: Whirlwind, Spear Cyclone, Earthquake, Shadow Step, Arcane Nova or Reaper's Pull.
  • Full combo then HEAVYGOD FINISHER: your god's own power - Zeus' lightning, Poseidon's wave, Hades' soul harvest, Athena's judgement and more.
  • HOLD HIT, then let goCHARGED ATTACK: Spin Attack, Piercing Charge, Cyclone, Fan of Blades, Arcane Beam or Death Whirl.
  • Sprint + HEAVYSHOULDER CHARGE: run into monsters and knock them flying.
  • BLOCK + HITSHIELD BASH: stuns the monster.
  • PERFECT PARRY / PERFECT DODGE, then HITRIPOSTE: a huge critical counter-attack.
  • GUARDING MONSTERS 🛡️Hit a monster too many times with light attacks and it raises its guard. Break it with HEAVY, a charged attack or a shield bash - or hit it from BEHIND (BACKSTAB = +60% damage).
  • STAMINAEvery attack uses a bit of the green stamina circle. Out of stamina = weak, slow attacks. Fight smart, not fast.
  • EVERY GOD FIGHTS DIFFERENTLYWith your god's own weapon your attacks carry his power: Zeus chains lightning, Poseidon shoots water jets, Hades steals life, Athena strikes with her shield, Apollo burns, Ares builds fury, Hermes zips to monsters, Artemis shoots arrows, Hephaestus melts the ground, Demeter grows vines, Dionysus fights "drunk", Hecate casts hexes.
  • FIGHTING STYLESYour weapon decides your style: BLADE MASTER (swords), SPEAR WARRIOR (spears, tridents - long reach), BERSERKER (axes, hammers - unstoppable), ASSASSIN (daggers - fast, critical hits), BATTLE MAGE (staffs - magic bolts and orbs), REAPER (scythe - heals with every hit). Find new weapons to change your style!
  • POWERYour god's special throw. When the gold bar is full, POWER becomes your god's ULTIMATE!
  • STARS OF OLYMPUSEach kingdom is a big island. Clear monster camps, race through rings, smash golden urns, climb the tower and find the treasure chest to win stars. 4 stars open the boss gate. Follow the golden arrow and the map.
  • BLUE JUMP PADSStep on them to fly high up to towers and floating islands.
  • COINSEvery 100 coins = 1 extra life.
  • CAMERAPhone: drag on the right side of the screen to turn and tilt, pinch with two fingers to zoom, or use the + / - buttons at the top. Computer: drag the mouse, use the mouse wheel, Z / C to turn.
  • RIDE CARLITO & BAGHEERAKnock the Beast Rider off the cat or the dog. Stand next to it and press RIDE. Riding: HIT = claw / bite combo, HEAVY = pounce, JUMP = big leap, DODGE = dash. Press RIDE again to jump off. Your pet stays on your team and fights next to you: Carlito claws, pounces, hisses and spins his tail; Bagheera bites, leaps and howls to scare monsters and heal you.
  • Red circle on the groundA big attack is coming. Get out!
  • Skeleton hoplitesThey block with shields. Use finishers, HEAVY slams or the plunge.
  • Flying birdsJump attacks and POWER throws hit them in the air.
  • The MinotaurDodge his charge - he crashes into the wall and gets dizzy. Attack him then!
  • Nemean Lion, Hydra, CyclopsLion: dodge his charge so he hits the wall. Hydra: cut heads, more grow back - keep going! Cyclops: watch the red circles, boulders land there.
  • MedusaWhen her eyes flash, DODGE or you turn to stone. Tap fast to break free.
  • Combo rankKeep hitting without getting hurt: D, C, B, A, S, SS... OLYMPIAN!
